## Research Overview  

The study analyzed the challenges and opportunities faced by long-distance bus 
transportation in China after the expansion of high-speed rail (HSR).  

## Methods  

- Data scraping of timetables for bus and high-speed rail services  
- Mode choice modeling with non-aggregated passenger data  
- Comparative analysis of service competitiveness  

## Findings  

- HSR diverted a significant portion of traditional bus passengers  
- Buses retained advantages in cost, accessibility, and regional coverage  
- Complementary roles identified between bus and HSR networks  

## Outputs  

- Undergraduate research paper in transport geography  
- Policy insights for integrated transport planning  
- Evidence for bus repositioning in evolving transport systems
