## Research Overview  

This research explored how land price varies across Guangzhou, focusing on both 
spatial patterns and underlying drivers. It aimed to provide a quantitative basis 
for understanding urban land market behavior.  

## Methods  

- Regression analysis of land price datasets  
- GIS-based spatial visualization of price patterns  
- Correlation with socio-economic and environmental indicators  

## Findings  

- Land prices showed strong clustering around central business districts  
- Accessibility and planning factors significantly explained variance  
- Social and environmental variables influenced peripheral markets  

## Outputs  

- Coursework report in urban economics  
- Land price maps for academic analysis  
- Contribution to policy discussions on spatial equity
