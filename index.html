Bio

Shasha Tian’s recent work uses large-scale mobility and user-generated data to examine how spatial structures and human mobility jointly shape unequal access to opportunities and environmental exposure. Her earlier research training on environment and transportation first developed the statistical and spatial-analytical foundations that now underlie her AI-enabled, data-driven approach.
